# Project Isekai (pre-alpha)

A cosy low-poly 3D action-life RPG for Windows. You're summoned into a round fantasy city that runs on the real clock and seasons. Fight and explore, rise through the Adventurer's Guild, fish and catch bugs, farm and cook, decorate a house and garden, dress up, and make friends with the townsfolk. It comes with **Isekai Studio**, for making your own content without code.

> **Pre-alpha:** expect bugs, placeholder art and music, and things that change between builds. Saves from one pre-alpha may not load in the next.

## Download

Get the latest zip from **[Releases](../../releases)**, unzip it anywhere, and run `Project Isekai/Project Isekai.exe`. The `README.md` and `docs/how-to-play.md` inside the zip explain the controls and your first day.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), and a graphics card that supports Vulkan or DirectX 12. You need about 800 MB of disk space. Keyboard and mouse or a gamepad both work.

## What's in this build

- **The world:** a city, plains, forest, coast, an island village and dungeons, with real-time seasons, weather, tides and moon phases.
- **Combat:** real-time combat with dodging, statuses, weapon mastery and skills, and 40 creatures, some of which only come out in certain weather, at night or at low tide.
- **Life:** fishing, bug catching, farming, cooking, pets, a museum, jobs, festivals and mail.
- **Your home:** a house and yard to decorate and paint, a workbench with plans, cabins, and a photo album.
- **Your look:** outfits for every slot, dyes, and a sewing lesson.
- **Little things to find:** returning rare creatures, mystery threads, mini-games (skating, kites, darts), footprints in the snow, and the Milky Way on dark nights.
- **Isekai Studio:** the content editor the game itself is built with.

## Known limits

- **Online play is off.** Online features (the Crossroads Room, friends, online co-op) need a server that isn't running yet, so they'll say they can't connect.
- **Placeholder art and sound.** Most art and all music and sound are placeholders.

## Reporting bugs

Please open an **[issue](../../issues)** saying what you were doing and what happened. Attach your error log if you can: in the game, Settings → **Open the logs folder**, then `errors.log`.
